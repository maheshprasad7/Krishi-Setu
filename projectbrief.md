# Project Brief — Krishi-Setu

## Overview
Krishi-Setu (formerly Agri-Mithra) is a **farmer-friendly, bilingual (Kannada + English) AI-powered AgriTech platform** built for rural farmers in Karnataka, India. It provides intelligent crop disease detection, voice-based farming advice, APMC market prices, and local weather forecasts — all in a simple, mobile-first interface.

---

## App Purpose
To bridge the technology gap between modern AI and Indian rural farmers by offering a localized, easy-to-use platform that delivers actionable agricultural guidance in the farmer's native language (Kannada).

---

## Goals
- Help farmers identify crop diseases using AI-powered leaf scanning
- Provide real-time farming advice via a voice assistant (Kannada + English)
- Show APMC market prices for produce in local mandis
- Display local weather forecasts relevant to farming decisions
- Offer government scheme information (PM-KISAN, Fasal Bima, Krishi Bhagya, etc.)
- Fully functional offline via a built-in fallback knowledge base

---

## Target Users
- **Primary**: Rural farmers in Karnataka (Kannada-speaking)
- **Secondary**: Agricultural extension workers, agri-tech enthusiasts
